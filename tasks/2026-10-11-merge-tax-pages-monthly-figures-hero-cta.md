# Merge the salary tax pages, add monthly figures and a retirement call to action

## Summary

- Replaced the eight `calc/take-home-salary-tax-ya<year>.html` pages with a single `calc/take-home-salary-tax.html`. The assessment year now comes from the `?ya=` query, so `calc/take-home-salary-tax.html?ya=2025` opens YA 2025 and an absent or unsupported query opens the newest enacted year, currently YA 2026, because YA 2027 is still a Budget proposal. Selecting a year keeps the entered details and rewrites the query, so year-specific links stay shareable; the document title, the proposal notice and the skip link follow the selection. The year-specific URLs now return 404 on Pages because no redirect stubs were kept.
- Added monthly figures to the "Where your money goes" breakdown. Each row now shows its monthly average next to its annual amount in a table, so the two columns stay aligned for every row, and the column headers carry the `(RM)` unit like the tax bracket table below. The existing note still states that monthly figures are annual averages rather than payslip deductions.
- Stopped narrow results from widening the page: the stacked workspace track is now `minmax(0, 1fr)` instead of `1fr`, whose automatic minimum is the content's min-content width. The breakdown table sits in the existing `.table-scroll` wrapper, so amounts wider than a small phone scroll inside the panel. Measured in Chromium at 320, 375, 768 and 1440 pixels: no page overflow up to a RM 1,000,000 monthly salary, where `main` widened the document to 332 pixels at 320 pixels, and the default breakdown fits 320 pixels without scrolling.
- Added a second hero call to action for the retirement calculator beside "Calculate your take-home pay". Both links sit in a wrapping `.hero-actions` row, and the retirement link uses a new `.button.secondary` outline variant, white on the existing teal border with a mint hover, so the tax calculator stays the primary path. Its label, "Plan your retirement", matches the retirement tool card and keeps both buttons on one line at 1440 pixels; below that they stack, and at 320 pixels each fills the column without widening the page. The browser suite asserts that both hero calls to action target the two calculators and resolve with a 200 response.
- Updated every internal link, the browser suite and README. The suite now loads 6 routes, including `?ya=2022` and `?ya=2027`, asserts the year-specific title, selected option and proposal notice from the URL, and compares each breakdown row against the engine's annual amount and its twelfth. `noOverflow` compares against the requested viewport width because mobile emulation widens `innerWidth` to fit overflowing content, which hid the overflow this change removes. Tax rules, engine mathematics and the Pages packaging configuration are unchanged.
