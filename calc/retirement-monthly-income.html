What this estimate covers

This budget spends your savings over your chosen timeline and does not reserve a final balance or adjust for inflation, changing returns, taxes or unexpected expenses.

At 3% annual inflation, RM 1,000 buys about as much as RM 971 in today’s money after one year, and RM 943 after two years.

How the estimate works

Your savings remain invested while you draw an equal annual amount at the start of each year. We divide that amount by 12 to show a monthly budget.

Monthly figures are averages of annual withdrawals, so drawing a different amount each month may produce a different result. At a 0% return, savings are divided evenly across the retirement years, and any investment return is hypothetical.
